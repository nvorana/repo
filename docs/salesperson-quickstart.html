SalesCallOS is your personal coaching tool. Upload your sales calls and it reviews them against our Accelerator script — what you did well, what to tweak, and the one thing to work on next. It's here to help you handle objections better, sound more confident, and close more. Only you and your coach see your calls.

How it works

  1. Log in & go to “My Coaching.” It's your private space — just your calls.
  2. Upload a call. Type the client's name, then drop in the recording (mp3, m4a, wav…). That's it.
  3. Let it work (a few minutes). You'll see it transcribe and review in the background — you can leave and come back.
  4. Watch your progress. Your average score, your latest call, and your “Focus this week” — the one skill to level up.
  5. Get coached. Your coach reviews each call with you, then unlocks the full report and notes for you to keep.

The 🔒 you'll see

Right after uploading, a call shows a 🔒 “Pending your coach.” That's normal — your coach goes through it with you first, then releases it so you see the full report and feedback. The point is real coaching, not a number on a screen.

The daily habit

Upload your consultation calls at the end of each day. The reps who review their own calls are the ones who improve fastest — this makes that effortless.
