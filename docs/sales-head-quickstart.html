Sales Head — Quick Start

SalesCallOS reviews every one-on-one sales call against our Accelerator Program framework and hands you a coaching-ready report — score, what went right and wrong, the objections (even the unspoken ones), tonality, and a prioritized fix list. Your job: review, coach the rep, release.

Log in

Open: salesos.chillyonaryo.com  ·  Email + password (the ones set for you)  ·  Works on laptop or phone.

Your daily 10 minutes

  1. Open the Coaching queue. On Team Coaching, every completed call waiting for you sits in the queue with a count.
  2. Read the Summary first. Score out of 10, a one-line verdict, the rep's #1 focus, and their biggest win & biggest miss — the whole call in 10 seconds.
  3. Hear the key moments. Click any ▶ timestamp to jump the recording to that exact spot. Best coaching gold there is.
  4. Go deeper if needed. Tabs for Objections (unresolved first), Scorecard vs. our 12-part script, and Coaching priorities.
  5. Write your Coach's notes. Commend, correct, set the next focus — right at the top of the report.
  6. Coach the rep, then release. After your 1:1, tick Discussed and Release — only then can the rep see the report.

The golden rule

Coach first, release second. Reps cannot see their score or report until you release it — so the feedback always happens in your 1:1, not from a number read alone. The Awaiting coach → Coached ✓ badges show you what's still open.

One mindset

The score is a judgment, not a measurement — coach the trend over time, not a single number. A rep climbing from 4 → 6 → 7 is the win.
