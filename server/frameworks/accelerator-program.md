# Accelerator Program — Conversation Navigator

Calls reviewed under this framework are one-on-one consultation/closing calls
for the Accelerator Program (Negosyo University), typically conducted in
Taglish (mixed Tagalog/English). Quote the transcript in whichever language
was spoken; write the review itself in English.

The salesperson is expected to follow this 12-part structure. Judge the call
by how faithfully and skillfully they executed each part — order matters, and
so do the "shut up" moments.

## The 12 parts

1. **Small chat (2–3 min).** Build rapport: audio check, light personal
   questions, get their nickname, and ask permission to record. Should feel
   natural, not rushed or robotic.
2. **State the agenda & take the lead.** The three-step agenda: questions
   about the current situation → where they want to go → "I'll explain what I
   have to offer, then YOU decide." Must wait for an explicit OK before
   moving forward. This frames the prospect as the decision-maker.
3. **Figure out why they are here — the 3-level pain unfolding.** Prospects
   first give SURFACE answers ("okay naman po... pero kulang", "gusto ko lang
   ng extra"). These are decoys. The rep must hold space and probe with
   curious follow-ups ("Kulang saan?", "Anong ibig mong sabihin?") to reach
   LEVEL 2 (real leaks: "puro bayad lang, walang natitira", "walang savings,
   walang peace of mind") and ideally LEVEL 3, the emotional heart of the
   pain ("pagod na akong maging breadwinner", "ayokong abutan ng anak ko na
   ganito pa rin ako"). A rep who accepts a surface answer and moves on has
   failed this part.
4. **Understand their current situation.** Work, years employed, commute,
   salary (net or gross), health/family impact. The instruction is SHUT UP:
   let them talk, do not interrupt.
5. **Gather essential data & self-inflicted pain.** Debts and why they grew,
   failed businesses and money lost, why they can't save, and the
   consequence question: "What will happen kung hindi mo ma-solusyunan itong
   problema mo?" Again: let them talk.
6. **Figure out what they want.** Future-pace 3 years ahead. Must extract a
   SPECIFIC peso-per-month figure from the prospect (wait until they say a
   number), plus vivid lifestyle detail (car, vacation, family changes). If
   they aim too low, help them aim higher; case studies welcome here.
7. **Releasing control & self-admission.** "Why don't you put up an e-book
   business on your own?" Listen for the three magic statements: can't do it
   alone, want it faster, want a proven system with guidance. The prospect
   must say these — not the rep.
8. **Gaining commitment & wrapping in emotion.** "Why not just stay where you
   are?" → how does the problem affect their life → when do they want to fix
   it → how committed are they. This is where the emotional driver surfaces.
9. **Acknowledge the gap & ask permission to share.** Credibility (thousands
   coached, coach's track record) and an explicit "Would you like me to tell
   you more?"
10. **State what you are an expert at.** Mirror 2–3 pains and 2–3 struggles
    the prospect actually stated, so they think "that's me." Then state the
    promise (one-on-one coach, business built in 30 days or less) and SHUT UP.
11. **State the offer — high level, vague, outcome-focused.** No more than
    ~2 minutes. NO features, NO process detail, NO price. The 4-ebooks-a-day
    calculator moment belongs here. Answer only the specific questions asked,
    then shut up. Price is stated ONLY when the prospect asks for it.
12. **Incentive-based pricing & THE SILENCE.** Investment is P38,000 → if they
    join now, P29,000 (save P9,000), two-payment option of P14,500. After
    stating it: total silence until the prospect speaks — even if it takes
    many minutes. Caving in (talking first, justifying, discounting further,
    softening) is the cardinal sin of this framework. On a yes: collect
    payment method and a SPECIFIC deposit time/day; incentive price reserved
    only until a stated deadline.

## Non-negotiables

- The decision is BINARY. "Send me an email", "I'll think about it", "I need
  to ask my spouse" are not acceptable end-states — each has a prescribed
  handling script that converts it into either a real objection discussion or
  a specific time-bound decision deadline.
- Objection handling follows the scripts: "How much time do you need to
  decide?", "What haven't we discussed that you still need to think about?",
  "Isn't that why you came on the call to begin with!?", the spouse sign-off
  clarification (offer to get the spouse on a call), the trust reframe for
  program skeptics, the guarantee reframe, and the Magic Question (the real
  decision is about the life they want, not about joining us).
- "I have no money / can't afford it" is explored, not accepted: silence,
  empathy, "is this something you really want to do?", "how can we make this
  happen for you?"
- The rep should be detached from the YES ("release all attachment to the
  yes; attach to Accurate Diagnosis"). Pushiness that skips diagnosis to
  chase a close violates the spirit of the framework.

## What great execution sounds like

Long prospect turns during parts 3–8 (the rep asks short questions and
listens), a prospect-stated income target and emotional driver, the prospect
asking "how does it work?" and "how much?" unprompted, a cleanly delivered
incentive price followed by rep silence, and a binary outcome with a specific
payment time — or a clean, respectful no.

## Scorecard

- **Rapport & Frame**: Natural 2–3 minute small chat, recording permission asked, and the 3-step agenda stated with an explicit OK obtained before proceeding.
- **Pain Excavation (3 Levels)**: Probed past surface "okay lang pero kulang" answers to Level 2 practical leaks and Level 3 emotional pain; did not accept decoy answers.
- **Listening Discipline**: During discovery (parts 3–8) the rep asked short questions and let the prospect talk; no interrupting, no pitching, no monologues before the offer.
- **Vision & Specific Target**: Future-paced 3 years out and got a specific peso-per-month figure and vivid lifestyle detail from the prospect's own mouth.
- **Self-Admission & Commitment**: Elicited the magic statements (can't do it alone / want it faster / want a proven system) and surfaced the emotional driver and commitment level.
- **Outcome-Focused Offer**: Mirrored the prospect's stated pains ("that's me" effect), kept the offer under ~2 minutes, vague and outcome-focused, no features, no price until asked.
- **Pricing & The Silence**: Delivered incentive-based pricing exactly (P38,000 → P29,000 → two payments of P14,500) and held complete silence afterward without caving, justifying, or further discounting.
- **Binary Close & Objection Scripts**: Refused grey-area endings, used the prescribed objection scripts, and ended with a yes (specific payment method + deposit time/deadline) or a clean no — never a "think about it."
