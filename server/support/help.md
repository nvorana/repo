# CallCoach Help Notes

These notes ground the in-app support assistant. Keep them accurate; the
assistant answers only from here.

## Uploading a call
- Best accuracy: upload TWO separate audio files — your own recording and the
  client's recording (Zoom "record separate audio files per participant").
- You can still upload one combined file, but the app marks it "mixed audio"
  because it has to guess who spoke, so its talk-ratio and some findings are
  less reliable and it's excluded from team trends.
- Supported formats: mp3, m4a, wav, ogg, webm. Max size 250 MB.
- "No speech detected" usually means the wrong file (e.g. a muted track) —
  check you uploaded the right recordings.

## After uploading
- A review runs in stages (transcribing → analyzing) and can take a few minutes.
- Reps see their report only after their coach reviews and releases it.

## Re-analyzing
- A coach can re-run the AI on a call's transcript with the current scoring
  ("Re-analyze" on a report, or "Re-analyze all my calls" for their own calls).
  It doesn't re-transcribe; scores may change.

## Flagging inaccurate findings
- Under Wins & Misses, click "Flag as inaccurate" on any finding to tell the
  team the AI got it wrong. It doesn't change your report; it helps improve
  future reviews.

## Scores and trends
- Mixed-audio calls stay visible but don't count toward team or personal trends.

## Common issues
- Can't see a report: it may not be released by your coach yet.
- A finding cites a quote that isn't in the call: it's marked "quote not found
  in transcript" — read it with caution and flag it.
